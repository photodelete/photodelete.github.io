§ 3 Scope of Data Processing in the App

PhotoDelete is an app for locally cleaning up your photo and video library. The app requires access to your media library so that you can view, review, sort, keep, favorite, share, or select photos and videos for deletion.

The app processes in particular the following information locally on your device:

  • previews and displays of your photos and videos
  • creation and modification dates as well as available media metadata
  • file sizes, file names, MIME types, and storage folders as provided by Android’s MediaStore
  • image metadata (Exif), in particular camera manufacturer and model, where you open the file information sheet for a photo
  • media types such as photo, video, screenshot, or messenger media
  • swipe decisions, such as keep or delete
  • cleanup progress, for example by month or category
  • app settings, such as sorting options, reminder settings, or video sound status
  • information related to free usage, such as swipe counters or cooldown times

This data is processed locally on your device. Your photos and videos are not transferred to servers operated by the developer.

§ 4 Legal Bases

The processing of your data is based on the following legal bases:

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, where processing is necessary to provide the core functionality of the app.
  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, where you voluntarily grant an Android permission, such as access to your media library or permission to send notifications.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, where we have a legitimate interest in secure operation, support, and abuse prevention.

§ 5 Android Permissions

Media Access

PhotoDelete requests access to the photos and videos on your device in order to display them, read metadata such as date and file size, and create local cleanup sessions. Depending on your Android version, the following permissions are used:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES and READ_MEDIA_VIDEO on Android 13 and later
  • READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED on Android 14 and later, for access limited to selected media
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and earlier

Limited Media Access

Android allows you to grant PhotoDelete access only to selected photos and videos. In this case, PhotoDelete can only display and process the media you have shared with the app. You can extend, change, or revoke access at any time in the Android system settings under Apps, PhotoDelete, Permissions.

Notifications

On Android 13 and later, the app asks for the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission in order to send you optional cleanup reminders. Reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No reminder data is transmitted to the developer or to third parties. You can disable reminders at any time in the app settings or in the Android system settings.

Internet

The INTERNET permission is used exclusively for the processing of purchases and subscriptions as described in § 8. It is not used to transmit your photos, videos, or media metadata.

§ 6 Deleting, Favoriting, and Sharing

Deleting

If you select media for deletion, PhotoDelete uses the Android system function MediaStore.createTrashRequest. Android displays a system dialog that you must confirm. Confirmed media is moved to the Android system trash and, depending on your device and Android version, is typically removed permanently after around 30 days. Until then you can restore it in your gallery app.

Favoriting

If you mark media as a favorite, PhotoDelete uses the Android system function MediaStore.createFavoriteRequest. Depending on the item, Android may display a confirmation dialog. The favorite status is stored by Android in the MediaStore, not by the developer.

Sharing

If you share a photo or video, Android’s system share sheet opens and you choose the receiving app yourself. When sharing the throwback version of a photo, the app creates a temporary copy of the image with a caption in its own cache directory and provides it to the receiving app through a FileProvider. This copy is deleted the next time you share. When sharing without a caption, the original file is passed on unchanged. The developer does not receive a copy of shared media.

§ 7 No Transfer of Your Photos and Videos to Us

PhotoDelete does not transfer your photos or videos to the developer and does not operate its own server for processing your media library. Media processing takes place on your device through Android’s MediaStore.

PhotoDelete only reads media that Android has indexed locally on the device. Media that is only available in a cloud service and is not stored locally is not accessible to the app. If you use a cloud service such as Google Photos, that provider processes your photos and videos according to your settings and its own privacy policy. PhotoDelete has no independent access to such data beyond the local media permissions granted by Android.

§ 6 Löschen, Favorisieren und Teilen

Löschen

Wenn du Medien zum Löschen auswählst, nutzt PhotoDelete die Android-Systemfunktion MediaStore.createTrashRequest. Android zeigt dir einen Systemdialog, den du bestätigen musst. Bestätigte Medien wandern in den Android-Papierkorb und werden je nach Gerät und Android-Version in der Regel nach etwa 30 Tagen endgültig entfernt. Bis dahin kannst du sie in deiner Galerie-App wiederherstellen.

Favorisieren

Wenn du Medien als Favorit markierst, nutzt PhotoDelete die Android-Systemfunktion MediaStore.createFavoriteRequest. Android kann dabei je nach Datei einen Bestätigungsdialog anzeigen. Der Favoritenstatus wird von Android im MediaStore gespeichert, nicht vom Entwickler.

Teilen

Wenn du ein Foto oder Video teilst, öffnet sich die Systemauswahl von Android und du wählst die empfangende App selbst aus. Beim Teilen der Rückblick-Fassung eines Fotos erstellt die App eine temporäre Kopie des Bildes mit Bildunterschrift im eigenen Zwischenspeicher und gibt sie über einen FileProvider an die empfangende App weiter. Diese Kopie wird beim nächsten Teilen gelöscht. Beim Teilen ohne Bildunterschrift wird die Originaldatei unverändert weitergereicht. Der Entwickler erhält keine Kopie geteilter Medien.

PhotoDelete liest ausschließlich Medien, die Android lokal auf dem Gerät indexiert hat. Medien, die nur in einem Cloud-Dienst liegen und nicht lokal gespeichert sind, sind für die App nicht zugänglich. Wenn du einen Cloud-Dienst wie Google Fotos nutzt, verarbeitet dieser Anbieter deine Fotos und Videos nach deinen Einstellungen und seiner eigenen Datenschutzerklärung.
